hey fam. welcome to the first journal entry.

i built this site as a place to dump practice recaps, photos from team dinners, meet results, the whole vibe. the instagram is great for posts but i wanted somewhere we could write a little more and have it not disappear into the algorithm.

## what goes here

- **weekly recaps** — what happened at practice, who crushed it, dumb stuff that happened in the lane
- **events** — team dinners, travel, socials
- **meets** — results and photos
- **whatever else** — if it happened at swim club, it goes in the journal

## how to write an entry

(this part is for whoever takes over after me — hi future person.)

each entry is just a markdown file in `src/content/journal/`. copy this one as a template, change the frontmatter (the stuff at the top between the `---` lines), write whatever you want in markdown, push to github, and the site rebuilds automatically. the handoff doc in the repo has more detail.

see you at practice.
